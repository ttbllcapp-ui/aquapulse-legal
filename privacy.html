Privacy Policy · AquaPulse

Effective Date: June 1, 2025 · Last Updated: October 18, 2026

TTB International LLC ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how the AquaPulse: Water Tracker mobile application ("AquaPulse", the "App") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

📌 At a glance
  • Core features are free with no ads. AquaPulse Pro is an optional subscription for additional features; purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat.
  • You can use the app as a guest, with no account. Your water logs and profile are stored on your device.
  • Some features send data off your device: AquaCoach AI sends your messages and some profile data to our server and to a third-party AI provider (Section 1.3), and the App checks your subscription status with RevenueCat (Section 1.5).
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google, or use Family Mode, we collect the minimum data needed to run those features.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all data at any time from inside the app (Settings → "Permanently delete my account").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data stored only on your device (default for guests)

  • Profile metrics you enter during onboarding: weight, height, age, gender, country.
  • Daily water/beverage logs and cup customizations.
  • App preferences: language, theme, reminder schedule, sound toggle.
  • Local hydration streaks and achievements.

This data is stored on your device via AsyncStorage. We do not see it, except for the parts that are sent when you use AquaCoach (Section 1.3) or Family Mode (Section 1.4).

1.2 Data collected when you sign in (optional)

Where the App offers sign-in and you choose to sign in with Apple Sign In or Google Sign In, we receive from those providers and store on our servers:

  • A unique user ID (Sub claim)
  • Your email address
  • Your display name (if you choose to share it)
  • Your profile picture URL (if available)

If Apple does not share your email address with us, we create a placeholder identifier instead. Google sign-in is verified through an authentication service provided by Emergent, which passes your Google account email, name and profile picture URL to our server. We also store a sign-in session token so you stay signed in.

1.3 Data collected when you use AquaCoach AI

AquaCoach is an AI hydration coach included in AquaPulse Pro. When you send a message to AquaCoach, the App sends the following to our server:

  • The text of your message.
  • Your app language setting.
  • Context from your profile and today's progress, which the App adds automatically to each message so the coach can personalize its answer: weight, height, age, gender, daily water goal, how much you have had today and the percentage of your goal, the number of days in your streak, and your country.
  • An identifier: your account ID if you are signed in, or otherwise a random guest ID created on your device.

Third-party AI processing. To generate a reply, our server sends your message and the context above to a third-party AI model provider. Today this is Groq, Inc., running an open-weight Llama model. The provider processes the data only to produce the reply. Its handling of this data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy (Groq Privacy Policy). If we change the provider, we will update this policy. Please do not include information in your messages that you do not want processed this way, such as other health conditions or contact details.

Storage. Your messages and AquaCoach's replies are stored on our servers, linked to your account ID or guest ID, so the App can show your chat history. Your chat history is shown in the App only to the account or guest ID it belongs to. We also keep a daily count of messages per ID, and use your IP address briefly, to enforce usage limits and prevent abuse.

1.4 Data collected in Family Mode

  • Your daily water goal, today's intake, your percentage of the goal, and your streak length, shared only with members of your family group.
  • Your name (or your email address if no name is available) and profile picture, shown on the family leaderboard.
  • The family group invite code you created or joined.

1.5 Subscriptions and RevenueCat

AquaPulse Pro purchases are made through Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service, to process purchases and restores and to check whether you have an active AquaPulse Pro subscription. On iOS, the App connects to RevenueCat when it starts, even if you never buy anything, so it knows which features to unlock.

  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID that its software creates on your device. From version 1.2, the App also sends this ID to our server with AquaCoach messages so the server can confirm an active AquaPulse Pro subscription. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription information from Apple, such as the plan you bought, transaction and renewal dates, trial status, and whether the subscription will renew.
  • RevenueCat's software also collects technical information needed to provide its service, such as your device type and operating system version, app version, App Store country, and IP address.
  • We do not send RevenueCat your hydration logs, profile details, or AquaCoach messages.
  • Apple handles payment. We and RevenueCat never receive your payment card details.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for how RevenueCat handles this data.

1.6 Data we do NOT collect

  • No advertising IDs (IDFA / GAID).
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs (no Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.).
  • No HealthKit data in this version (HealthKit integration will be added in a future version with explicit user permission).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core hydration tracking experience.
  • To keep your account, family membership and AquaCoach history available when you sign in.
  • To process AquaPulse Pro purchases and unlock Pro features for active subscribers.
  • To deliver personalized AquaCoach AI responses.
  • To enable the optional Family Mode leaderboard.
  • To detect abuse and ensure service security.

We will never sell or rent your data to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

We share the minimum necessary data with the following service providers, who act on our behalf as data processors:

  • Apple: App Store payments for AquaPulse Pro, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.
  • Google and Emergent: Google sign-in, if you choose to use it. Emergent's authentication service verifies the sign-in and passes your email, name and profile picture URL to our server.
  • Groq, Inc. (AI model provider): to generate AquaCoach AI replies. Your message text, language setting and the profile context described in Section 1.3 are sent.
  • Render: hosts our backend server, which handles sign-in, AquaCoach and Family Mode requests.
  • MongoDB Atlas (cloud database): to store your account, AquaCoach chats, and family data.
  • RevenueCat: processes AquaPulse Pro purchases and subscription status on our behalf, as described in Section 1.5. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or for marketing purposes.

4. Children's Privacy

AquaPulse is rated 4+ but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have done so, please contact us at info@ttbinternationalllc.com and we will delete the data immediately.

5. Data Retention

  • Guest local data: stays on your device until you uninstall or reset the app.
  • Account data: retained while your account is active. Deleted permanently within 7 days when you press "Permanently delete my account".
  • AquaCoach chat history: kept until you delete it. You can delete it anytime by tapping the trash icon in the AI Coach tab. Changing the app language also clears it. Deleting your account deletes the chat history linked to your account; chat history from before you signed in is linked to your guest ID, so delete it with the trash icon.
  • Subscription records: Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies. Deleting your AquaPulse account does not cancel your subscription or delete these records. To ask us to delete your RevenueCat data, email us.

6. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA, KVKK)

You have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of your data.
  • Correct: fix inaccurate data via Settings.
  • Delete: erase your account in-app (Settings → delete account). Without an account, use Settings → "Delete my data" to erase your AquaCoach chat history from our server and all data on your device. You can also email us.
  • Object / Restrict: limit certain processing by signing out, staying as a guest, or not using AquaCoach.
  • Portability: request your data in a machine-readable JSON format by email.

To exercise any right, email info@ttbinternationalllc.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We use industry-standard practices: HTTPS for all network traffic, encrypted database storage, token-based authentication, and secure password-less sign in via Apple/Google. No method is 100% secure but we strive to use commercially acceptable means.

8. International Transfers

Our servers are located in the United States and Europe. By using AquaPulse, you consent to your data being transferred to these jurisdictions.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be announced inside the app and the "Last Updated" date above will change. Continued use after a change means you accept the new policy.
